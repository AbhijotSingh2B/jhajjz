# Storyboard v2: Jhajj'z, three cars (15.0 s, 1080×1920, 60 fps)

v2 after storyboard critic 1 (review/storyboard-critic-1.md): text cut from 61 to ~43 words, one shot per car with a
different composition each, end card from 11.7 s (3.3 s), all city dots together, no physically impossible move.

**Idea:** the same bright studio (paper cyclorama, polished sand floor) for every car, one shot per car, the camera
always travelling right to left so the cuts match in direction. Then all three together on one floor, where they
work, and how to ask.

**Beat grid:** cuts on the music's beat. Placeholder grid 123 BPM (beat 0.488 s): cuts at 1.46, 3.41, 5.37, 7.32, 9.76,
11.71 s (beats 3, 7, 11, 15, 20, 24). Re-timed to the chosen track.

**Title position:** every car name sits in the same place: left-aligned at x 72, baseline about y 400, Caslon 96 px,
with the years under it in DM Sans 40 px. Everything stays inside x 72–1008, y 250–1500.

## Three signature moments
1. A soft band of light runs down the Ghost's bonnet while the camera draws back to show the whole car.
2. The Ghost's name grows past the camera and the S-Class is already waiting behind it.
3. The camera lifts back from the Range Rover and the other two cars are parked behind it on the same floor.

## On mute
By 15 s a viewer has read who (Jhajj'z, since 1998, chauffeur-driven), which three cars, what they're booked for,
where (head office in Chandigarh, pan-India) and what to do (WhatsApp +91 98036 00001).
